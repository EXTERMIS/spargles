SPARGL: Audio Lab

Upload, sculpt, preview and export sound

SPARGL: Audio Lab is a sound editor that runs entirely in your browser. Drop in an audio file, shape it with a full rack of effects or a one-click preset, preview the result against the original, and export it when you're happy. Everything stays in your browser, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

SPARGL: Audio Lab

Everything stays in your browser

One-Click Presets

Start from a preset instead of a blank rack. More than 80 presets are sorted into nine categories, with a search box to find the one you want.

  • Voice & Comms
  • Spaces & Reverb
  • Creatures & Sci-Fi
  • Lo-Fi & Retro
  • Time & Echo, Modulation & Movement
  • Music Mixing, Distortion & Grit, Mastering & Utility

A Full Effects Rack

Every effect is a panel of sliders, and you can type an exact value next to any of them. Collapse the panels you aren't using.

  • Volume, pan, speed / pitch and fades
  • Trim, noise gate, normalize and reverse
  • EQ, filters and de-essing
  • Distortion, bit-crush and tube warmth
  • Echo, reverb, chorus, phaser and vibrato
  • Stereo tools, ring mod and dynamics

Preview and Export

See the waveform, play, loop or reverse it, and flip to the A/B original to hear exactly what your effects are doing. When you're done, export in the format you need.

  • MP3 at 128 to 320 kbps
  • WAV at 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float
  • Stereo or mono, with your choice of sample rate
  • Save processed sounds to your browser library
